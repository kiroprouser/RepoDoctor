# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Repo Doctor welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep project spaces constructive and considerate.

Examples of behavior that supports the community include:

- offering specific, respectful feedback;
- discussing ideas and technical trade-offs without personal attacks;
- accepting corrections and focusing on what helps the project;
- making space for questions and first-time contributions.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without their permission.

## Applying these expectations

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not meet these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary. If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository's GitHub contact or reporting feature. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports.

This policy applies in project discussions, reviews, issues, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
